WHAT CHANGED
  Lift: clock at the left corner with the floor indicator beside it, looping word
        and line under it; everything moved up so the panel opens over empty space.
  Flower: an architectural pillar holding flowers in the visitor's colour, right side only.
  Floor buttons: numbers only (G,1,2,3), with a lit light for the current floor.
  Menu: Our Services, Membership, Join the Network, Announcements, Exit, in the same
        floating panel under the floors. Each opens a popup (words: data/popups.js).
        Membership wording is a DRAFT; Join, Services, Announcements and Exit are placeholders.
  Marquee: words centred in a thinner band, 45 seconds a pass, left to right from the logo circle.
  Desktop: a wing each side of the lift (32/36/32) with a vertical EXPLORE control on its
        outer edge. Wings are empty (a quiet ornament) until entries are added. They appear
        only after the lift doors open and change with the floor.
  Phone: the lift is a little narrower so a sliver of each wing shows with a glowing arrow.
        Tap the sliver or swipe to bring the wing in full width; tap the floor badge at the
        top (or swipe back) to return to the lift.
  Desktop only: the building behind the lift is quieter.

STILL TO COME: time-of-day lift walls, real Membership and Join popups,
  one small file for the looping lift messages.
